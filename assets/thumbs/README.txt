YouTube thumbnail wall (shown at low opacity behind the profile on the main page)

Drop your thumbnails in this folder named by number:
  1.jpg  2.jpg  3.jpg  4.jpg ...   (png / webp / jpeg also work)

They are picked up automatically, in order, and stop at the first missing number.
16:9 images look best. 6-12 thumbnails fill the wall nicely; more is fine.
To use custom file names instead, list them in THUMBS at the bottom of js/main.js.
