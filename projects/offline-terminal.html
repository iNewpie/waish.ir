Offline Terminal

The assistant living in this site. Commands start with /, everything else goes to a local knowledge base — in English or Persian.

How it works

Everything runs in the browser. Anything you type that starts with / is a command; anything else is matched against a knowledge base I maintain myself, and the answer fades in word by word. Write in Persian and it answers in Persian.

What it knows

Who I am, LunaMC, ClutchPing, my content, my full PC setup, and a set of computer-science basics — TCP, UDP, HTTP, DNS, ping, RAM, processes vs threads, Big-O.
