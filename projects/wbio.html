wbio.ir

One link for every social. A bio-link page builder made for Iran — themes, live game cards, effects, music and your own domain, running entirely on Cloudflare Workers.

Why

Every creator, streamer and shop in Iran puts the same handful of links in every bio — Instagram, Telegram, Discord, YouTube — and most link-in-bio tools are blocked, slow, or want a foreign card. wbio.ir gives you wbio.ir/you: one fast page that opens everywhere, with Persian as the first language, not an afterthought.

What you get

An avatar, a bio, links and social icons in a minute. Then it grows with you: 20 themes with their own layouts, colours and fonts, GIF avatars and backgrounds, 23 effects, a music player, a custom badge, and live cards that pull your Valorant rank, Minecraft server status or Discord presence onto the page. Analytics show views and clicks per link, and referral codes pay out for the friends you bring.

Your own domain

On the Enterprise plan the page lives on bio.yourname.com. You add one DNS record — directly or through ArvanCloud or Cloudflare — and wbio handles the certificate and the routing. No Cloudflare for SaaS, no international card: the domain lands on my own origin, which hands it to the worker.

How it is built

A single Cloudflare Worker with a Durable Object (SQLite) for users, pages, files and analytics — zero servers to babysit and no running cost until it earns. Passwordless sign-in by email code, Discord or GitHub; payments through Zarinpal; every response hardened with a strict CSP. The dashboard is a live editor: what you change shows in the phone preview before you save.
